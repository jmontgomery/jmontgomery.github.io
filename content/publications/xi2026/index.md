summary: 'Introduces the Big Five Adaptive Inventory (BF-AI), a freely available computerized adaptive test built on the 100-item IPIP-NEO via the catSurv R package. In simulation studies the BF-AI outperforms established short forms in precision, with five adaptive items matching IPIP-50 accuracy. A criterion validity study (n=3,029) shows the BF-AI predicts seven life outcomes at least as well as fixed alternatives, substantially reducing the brevity–precision trade-off without increasing respondent burden.'
