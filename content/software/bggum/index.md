summary: 'R package for Bayesian estimation of the generalized graded unfolding model (GGUM) via Metropolis-coupled MCMC. Recovers ideal points in settings where respondents with opposing extreme preferences respond identically — the "ends against the middle" problem.'
abstract: 'Standard IRT models assume that higher trait levels always produce higher endorsement probabilities — an assumption that fails for "ends against the middle" items, where respondents with opposing extreme preferences respond identically. `bggum` implements the generalized graded unfolding model (GGUM) via Metropolis-coupled Markov chain Monte Carlo, recovering ideal points even when opposites respond alike. Includes post-processing, parameter estimation, and plotting utilities. Companion to Duck-Mayr and Montgomery (2023), *Political Analysis* (Warren Miller Prize, best paper in *Political Analysis*).'
